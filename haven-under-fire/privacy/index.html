Privacy policy

Updated 24 November 2026 · Oberasoftware

Haven Under Fire has no advertising, tracking, analytics service, third-party account or game backend. We do not receive your gameplay data automatically.

What stays on your device

Your campaign, Custom Game, settings and performance counters stay on your device. Nothing advances while the game is closed. Deleting the app removes its local saves. Restore Purchases restores ownership, not a deleted settlement.

Purchases through Apple

Optional purchases use Apple’s StoreKit. Apple processes payment and Apple Account information under Apple’s privacy policy. The game reads verified purchase status to unlock Full Game. It does not receive payment-card details. StoreKit’s on-device transaction history supports offline ownership; a new purchase or explicit restore needs Apple’s services.

Reports you choose to share

Feedback reports are optional. The game copies a report only when you ask. Game reports include gameplay counters; performance reports additionally include your device model and operating-system version. Review what you share before sending it. Nothing is sent automatically.

When you contact support

If you email support, we receive the message and contact details you choose to send and use them to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or payment-card details. Contact support@oberasoftware.com about your correspondence or to request its deletion.

Visiting this website

This website is separate from the game. These publisher pages do not use analytics, advertising cookies, embedded social widgets or third-party fonts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s hosting information and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to Apple, GitHub and other project sites take you to services with their own privacy practices. See our website privacy page for the scope of this policy.
