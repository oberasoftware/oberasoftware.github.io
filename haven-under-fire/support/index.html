How do I start?

Open Campaign, choose a difficulty and tap Willow Meadows. Build your Settlement HQ, then follow the Stone, storage and food tasks. Choosing a building creates a free preview; only Build here spends materials.

What is free, and what does Full Game unlock?

At launch, Willow Meadows is one complete free land. A single Full Game purchase unlocks seven more campaign lands through victories and Custom Game. The planned euro launch price is €3.99; your App Store shows the local price. No ads, subscriptions or paid resources.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Full Game & Restore from the main menu, then choose Restore purchases with the same Apple Account. Restoration recovers ownership, not deleted local saves. If approval is pending, wait for Apple to complete it before trying again.

Why is my food empty?

Greenhouses grow Crops. Community Kitchens turn delivered Crops into Meals, which couriers bring to a depot. Tap Meals to see production, consumption and delivery. A food shortage slows some industry; it does not shut off your towers.

Why is my tower not firing?

Check its power district, assigned operator and ammunition. Disconnected power districts cannot share spare generation. Missing inputs or a full warehouse can also delay ammunition. Some enemies can attack from beyond a tower’s range; combine defenses and research upgrades.

Where is my saved game?

Saves stay on your device, with separate slots for campaign and Custom Game. Continue resumes your last healthy autosave; no attacks happen while the game is closed. Restart resets the current land but keeps your best results. Deleting the app removes its local progress.

Why can’t I hear any sound?

Turn off iPhone Silent Mode, raise media volume and check Bluetooth or AirPlay output. In Settings, turn off the game’s mute switch and try a sound-check button. The game intentionally respects Silent Mode.

How do I report a problem?

Email support@oberasoftware.com with your device, iOS version, game version and steps to reproduce it. You can copy a game or performance report from the game and review it before sharing. Nothing is sent automatically. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

What if my device gets warm?

Try 30 FPS in Settings. If the issue continues, tell us your device model and what was happening in the game so we can investigate.

Who handles refunds?

Apple handles App Store payments and refund requests. Visit Apple’s purchase support for help. Oberasoftware does not receive your payment-card details.
