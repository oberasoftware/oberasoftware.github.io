Website privacy.

What happens when you visit these publisher pages.

Updated 24 November 2026

Scope

This policy covers the Oberasoftware Publishing homepage and the new product, support and privacy pages on www.oberasoftware.com. Haven Under Fire has a separate game privacy policy. Older project documentation and external services linked from this site may have different practices.

No tracking added by us

These publisher pages do not use advertising, analytics, cookies, local storage, embedded social widgets or external fonts. Viewing a screenshot does not send a feedback report or create an account.

Hosting with GitHub Pages

GitHub Pages serves this website. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security, whether or not you are signed in to GitHub. See GitHub’s hosting documentation and its Privacy Statement.

Contacting us

Email links open your email application. If you send us a message, we receive the contact details and information you choose to include, and use them to respond. Please do not include passwords or payment-card information. Email support@oberasoftware.com about your correspondence or to request its deletion.

Links to other services

Apple handles App Store purchases. GitHub hosts code, issues and documentation. When you follow a link to one of these services, its privacy practices apply. We do not collect your Apple Account credentials or payment-card details through this website.
